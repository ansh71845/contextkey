POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

ContextKey is an experimental, local-first context wallet. This policy explains what the website and app do with information.

1. Vault information stays in your browser

Vault entries and the encrypted vault record are stored in browser storage on the device where you use ContextKey. The app does not send vault entries, passphrases, encryption keys, or context-pack contents to a ContextKey application server. ContextKey currently has no account system, cloud vault, or server-side AI processing.

2. Information you choose to export or share

Context packs and portable JSON exports are readable files, not encrypted vault backups. Anyone who obtains an exported file may be able to read it. If you paste a context pack into an AI assistant or another service, that service handles the information under its own terms and privacy policy. Review the contents before exporting or sharing.

3. Encrypted backups

Encrypted vault backups are protected using encryption in your browser. Keep your passphrase private and store backup files carefully. Losing the passphrase may make a backup unrecoverable.

4. Website hosting and technical requests

The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. When your browser requests website files, GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser details, and requested resources under its own privacy documentation. ContextKey does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party marketing scripts to the app.

5. Device and browser risks

Local storage is not protection against a compromised device, malicious browser extensions, malicious scripts running in the same website origin, or someone using an unlocked browser profile. Lock your vault when finished and keep a separate encrypted backup.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the experimental product develops. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

7. Contact

For product or policy questions, use the ContextKey GitHub issue tracker. Do not post passphrases, vault exports, or private personal information in public issues.
